# NASA High Efficiency Centrifugal Compressor (HECC)

## Purpose

Experimental validation of TurboCompGPU centrifugal
compressor models.

## Validation objectives

- Total-to-total pressure ratio
- Compressor efficiency
- Corrected mass flow
- Corrected rotational speed
- Diffuser pressure recovery
- Operating characteristic predictions

## Current status

Dataset identification and source audit pending.

## Directories

- publications/: Published papers and technical reports
- raw/: Unmodified source data
- geometry/: Published compressor geometry
- processed/: Documented data transformations
- provenance/: Sources, checksums, and licensing information

## Validation requirements

Before comparing predictions against measurements:

1. Identify experimental measurement stations.
2. Establish the working-fluid composition and inlet conditions.
3. Confirm definitions of corrected flow and corrected speed.
4. Determine whether measured pressure ratios include
   the collector or volute.
5. Identify geometry and loss parameters unavailable
   from published sources.
6. Record measurement uncertainty where available.

Do not infer missing geometry or empirical coefficients
from the validation measurements.
